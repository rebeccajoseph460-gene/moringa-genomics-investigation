# Literature Review

This section contains summaries of scientific literature relevant to the nutritional, medicinal, and genomic characteristics of Moringa oleifera.

## Topics Covered

- Nutritional properties
- Medicinal properties
- Genomic resources
- Candidate genes
- Biological pathways

## Purpose

The literature review supports the identification and characterization of genes associated with nutritional and medicinal traits in Moringa oleifera.
