# Literature Review

## Introduction

This section contains summaries of scientific literature relevant to the nutritional, medicinal, and genomic characteristics of *Moringa oleifera*.

## Search Strategy

A literature search was conducted to identify scientific information relevant to *Moringa oleifera*. The search focused on the following thematic areas: Nutritional properties, Medicinal properties, Genomic resources, Candidate genes, Biological pathways

These themes were used to guide the selection and review of scientific articles, genomic databases, and bioinformatics resources relevant to the objectives of this study.

## Topics Covered

- Nutritional properties
- Medicinal properties
- Genomic resources
- Candidate genes
- Biological pathways

## Purpose

The literature review supports the identification and characterization of genes associated with nutritional and medicinal traits in *Moringa oleifera* and provides the scientific foundation for subsequent bioinformatics analyses.
