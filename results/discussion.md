# Discussion

## Nutritional Traits

The identification of genes associated with mineral transport,
protein biosynthesis, lipid metabolism, and carbohydrate metabolism
suggests that these biological processes may contribute to the
nutritional value of Moringa oleifera.

## Medicinal Traits

Genes involved in sterol metabolism, secondary metabolite
biosynthesis, and glycosylation processes were identified.
These functions are commonly associated with plant bioactive
compounds and may contribute to the reported medicinal
properties of Moringa oleifera.

## Study Limitations

The findings were based on computational predictions and
functional annotation. Experimental validation is required
to confirm the roles of the identified candidate genes.
