## Summary

Functional annotation identified candidate genes involved in:

- Mineral transport
- Energy metabolism
- Protein biosynthesis
- Sterol metabolism
- Secondary metabolite biosynthesis
- Plant defence and signalling processes

These genes represent promising targets for understanding the nutritional and medicinal properties of *Moringa oleifera* and will be further investigated through pathway analysis.
