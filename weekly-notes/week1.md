# Week 1 Progress Report

## Project

Exploring the Genomic Basis of Nutritional and Medicinal Traits in Moringa oleifera: A Bioinformatics Investigation

## Objectives Completed

- Developed the project proposal.
- Defined the research question and objectives.
- Conducted a preliminary literature review.
- Reviewed nutritional properties of Moringa oleifera.
- Reviewed medicinal properties of Moringa oleifera.
- Examined available genomic and transcriptomic resources.
- Selected a reference genome and transcriptomic dataset.

## Key Findings

### Nutritional Traits

Important nutritional components identified include:

- Proteins
- Essential amino acids
- Vitamin A precursors
- Vitamin C
- Iron
- Calcium
- Potassium

### Medicinal Traits

Major bioactive compounds identified include:

- Flavonoids
- Phenolic compounds
- Glucosinolates
- Isothiocyanates
- Terpenoids
- Alkaloids

### Genomic Resources

Reference genome selected:

- Species: *Moringa oleifera*
- Cultivar: Bhagya
- Assembly: ASM2139783v1
- Accession: GCA_021397835.1

Transcriptomic resource selected:

- SRR5834758
- Young leaf tissue

## Challenges Encountered

- Learning how genomic resources are organized within NCBI.
- Understanding the differences between genome assemblies and transcriptomic datasets.
- Exploring large annotation files and identifying relevant biological information.

## Lessons Learned

- Literature review provides an essential foundation for genomic investigations.
- A clear biological question should guide bioinformatics analyses.
- Nutritional and medicinal traits are often associated with secondary metabolism and biosynthetic pathways.

## Resources Created

- Project proposal
- Nutritional summary
- Medicinal summary
- Genomic resources review
- References file

Week 2 will focus on:

The literature review phase has established a strong foundation for the bioinformatics investigation. Key nutritional and medicinal traits of *Moringa oleifera* have been identified, along with genomic resources that will support candidate gene discovery and functional analysis.
