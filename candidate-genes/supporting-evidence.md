# Supporting Evidence

## Rationale for Candidate Gene Selection

Candidate genes were selected based on:

1. Published reports of nutritional and medicinal properties of *Moringa oleifera*.
2. Known biosynthetic pathways involved in nutrient accumulation.
3. Known pathways involved in flavonoid and phenolic synthesis.
4. Antioxidant defense mechanisms commonly studied in medicinal plants.

## Expected Outcomes

The RNA-seq workflow aims to:

- Identify expressed genes in Moringa.
- Detect genes associated with nutritional traits.
- Detect genes associated with medicinal traits.
- Prioritize genes for future functional validation.

## Current Limitation

These candidate genes are currently literature-based hypotheses and have not yet been confirmed in the analyzed RNA-seq dataset.
