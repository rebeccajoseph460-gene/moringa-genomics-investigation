# Nutritional Trait Candidate Genes

*Moringa oleifera* is recognized for its high nutritional value, including proteins, vitamins, minerals, and antioxidant compounds.

## Amino Acid and Protein Biosynthesis

### Glutamine Synthetase (GS)

Function:

- Nitrogen assimilation
- Amino acid biosynthesis
- Protein accumulation

Potential significance:

May contribute to the high protein content reported in Moringa leaves.

### Glutamate Synthase (GOGAT)

Function:

- Nitrogen metabolism
- Amino acid production

Potential significance:

Works together with GS in plant nitrogen utilization.

## Vitamin-Related Pathways

### Phytoene Synthase (PSY)

Function:

- Carotenoid biosynthesis

Potential significance:

May influence provitamin A accumulation.

## Mineral Utilization

### Nitrate Reductase (NR)

Function:

- Nitrate assimilation

Potential significance:

Influences nutrient utilization efficiency and plant growth.

Expression levels of these genes will be examined following annotation and RNA-seq quantification.
