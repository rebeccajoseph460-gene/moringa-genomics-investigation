# Nutritional Trait Candidate Genes

Several candidate genes with potential roles in nutrient transport, energy metabolism, carbohydrate metabolism, and protein biosynthesis were identified through functional annotation and pathway analysis.

---

## Molybdate Transporter of MFS Superfamily

**Gene ID**
- JAJFZO010000094.1.g155

**Function**

- Facilitates the transport of molybdenum across cellular membranes.
- Supports the movement and availability of an essential plant micronutrient.

**Potential Significance**

Molybdenum is required for several metabolic processes in plants. Efficient transport of this mineral may contribute to nutrient uptake, metabolism, and overall plant nutritional quality.

---

## FAD-dependent Glycerol-3-phosphate Dehydrogenase Family

**Gene ID**
- JAJFZO010000094.1.g159

**Function**

- Participates in lipid metabolism.
- Supports cellular energy production.
- Contributes to membrane biosynthesis.

**Associated Pathways**
- Glycerophospholipid Metabolism (ko00564)
- Biosynthesis of Secondary Metabolites (ko01110)

**Potential Significance**

This gene may contribute to the nutritional value of *Moringa oleifera* through its role in energy metabolism, lipid utilization, and maintenance of cellular functions.

---

## Class-I Aminoacyl-tRNA Synthetase Family

**Gene ID**
- JAJFZO010000094.1.g180

**Function**

- Essential for protein synthesis.
- Attaches amino acids to transfer RNA molecules during translation.

**Associated Pathway**
- Aminoacyl-tRNA Biosynthesis (ko00970)

**Potential Significance**

Protein biosynthesis is fundamental to plant growth and development. This gene may contribute to the high nutritional value of *Moringa oleifera* by supporting efficient protein production.

---

## Gluconokinase GntK/GntV Family

**Gene ID**
- JAJFZO010000094.1.g445

**Function**

- Participates in carbohydrate metabolism.
- Supports energy generation and carbon utilization.

**Associated Pathways**
- Pentose Phosphate Pathway (ko00030)
- Carbon Metabolism (ko01200)
- Metabolic Pathways (ko01100)

**Potential Significance**

This gene plays a role in energy production and nutrient utilization, both of which are important for growth and the accumulation of nutritional compounds.

---

## Associated Nutritional Pathways

### Aminoacyl-tRNA Biosynthesis (ko00970)

This pathway is essential for protein production and amino acid utilization, contributing to plant growth and nutritional quality.

### Glycerophospholipid Metabolism (ko00564)

This pathway is involved in lipid metabolism and cellular energy production, supporting normal plant development and nutrient utilization.

### Pentose Phosphate Pathway (ko00030)

This pathway generates metabolic intermediates required for biosynthesis and cellular maintenance.

### Carbon Metabolism (ko01200)

Carbon metabolism provides energy and biochemical building blocks necessary for plant growth and nutrient accumulation.

### Mineral Transport

Transport systems such as the molybdate transporter support the uptake and distribution of essential micronutrients within the plant.

---

## Summary

The nutritional trait candidate genes identified in this study are primarily involved in:

- Mineral transport
- Protein biosynthesis
- Energy metabolism
- Lipid metabolism
- Carbohydrate metabolism
- Carbon utilization

Together, these genes provide biological evidence supporting the nutritional value of *Moringa oleifera* and represent promising targets for future functional genomics investigations.
