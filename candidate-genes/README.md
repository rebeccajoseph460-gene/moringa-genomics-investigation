# Candidate Genes

This directory contains information on candidate genes potentially associated with nutritional and medicinal traits in *Moringa oleifera*.

The genes listed here were selected based on published literature regarding plant metabolism, nutrient biosynthesis, antioxidant activity, stress response, and secondary metabolite production. They serve as initial targets for investigation and may be refined following RNA-seq analysis, gene annotation, and differential expression analysis.

## Objectives

- Identify genes associated with nutritional traits.
- Identify genes associated with medicinally relevant phytochemicals.
- Evaluate gene expression patterns from RNA-seq data.
- Prioritize candidate genes for future functional studies.
