# Exploring the Genomic and Transcriptomic Basis of Nutritional and Medicinal Traits in *Moringa oleifera*: A Genomics and RNA-Seq Analysis Project

## Background

*Moringa oleifera* is widely recognized for its exceptional nutritional value and diverse medicinal applications. The plant is rich in proteins, vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, flavonoids, phenolic compounds, glucosinolates, and other bioactive metabolites that contribute to its increasing importance in food, health, and agricultural systems.

Although numerous nutritional and therapeutic benefits have been reported, the molecular mechanisms underlying these traits are not yet fully understood. Advances in genomics, transcriptomics, and bioinformatics now provide opportunities to investigate the genes and biological pathways that contribute to nutrient biosynthesis, antioxidant activity, secondary metabolism, and other beneficial characteristics.

By combining publicly available genomic resources with RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) data, this project aims to explore candidate genes and pathways that may contribute to the nutritional and medicinal properties of *Moringa oleifera*.

---

## Research Question

Which candidate genes and biological pathways contribute to the nutritional and medicinal traits of *Moringa oleifera*?

---

## Aim

To investigate the genomic and transcriptomic basis of nutritional and medicinal traits in *Moringa oleifera* using publicly available genomic resources, RNA-seq data, and bioinformatics approaches.

---

## Objectives

### General Objective

To identify and characterize candidate genes associated with nutritional and medicinal traits in *Moringa oleifera*.

### Specific Objectives

- Review published evidence on the nutritional and medicinal properties of *Moringa oleifera*.
- Investigate genomic and transcriptomic resources available for *Moringa oleifera*.
- Identify candidate genes associated with nutritional traits.
- Identify candidate genes associated with medicinal properties.

## Working Hypothesis

Genes involved in nutrient biosynthesis, antioxidant defense, secondary metabolism, and the production of bioactive compounds contribute to the nutritional and medicinal properties of *Moringa oleifera*.

## Data Resources

### Reference Genome

- Species: *Moringa oleifera*
- Cultivar: Bhagya
- Assembly: ASM2139783v1
- Accession: GCA_021397835.1

### RNA-Seq Dataset

- Accession: SRR5834758
- Tissue: Young leaf tissue
- Sequencing Type: Paired-end RNA sequencing

These publicly available datasets will serve as the primary resources for genomic and transcriptomic analyses in this project.

## Methodology

The project will follow a bioinformatics workflow consisting of the following stages:

### 1. Literature Review

- Review published studies on the nutritional and medicinal properties of *Moringa oleifera*.
- Identify compounds, pathways, and biological processes frequently associated with these traits.

### 2. Candidate Gene Identification

- Compile literature-supported candidate genes related to:
  - Nutrient metabolism
  - Protein biosynthesis
  - Antioxidant activity
  - Secondary metabolite production
  - Flavonoid and phenolic biosynthesis

### 3. Genome Resource Preparation

- Obtain the selected *Moringa oleifera* reference genome.
- Prepare genomic resources for downstream analyses.

### 4. RNA-Seq Analysis

- Align RNA-seq reads to the reference genome using HISAT2.
- Assess alignment quality using SAMtools.
- Process alignment files for downstream analyses.

### 5. Genome Annotation

- Generate gene annotations using computational gene prediction tools.
- Produce annotation files suitable for transcriptomic analyses.

### 6. Gene Expression Analysis

- Quantify gene expression using predicted annotations.
- Generate expression datasets for biological interpretation.

### 7. Functional Interpretation

- Investigate biological pathways associated with identified candidate genes.
- Relate findings to nutritional and medicinal traits reported in the literature.

### 8. Documentation and Reporting

- Document workflows and results.
- Maintain a project repository for reproducibility.
- Prepare a final project report summarizing findings and recommendations.
