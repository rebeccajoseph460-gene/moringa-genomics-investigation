# Moringa Genomics Project

Exploring the genomic and transcriptomic basis of nutritional and medicinal traits in *Moringa oleifera* using bioinformatics and RNA-seq analysis.

## Project Overview

*Moringa oleifera* is widely recognized for its nutritional value and medicinal applications. This project investigates the genes and biological pathways that may contribute to these traits using publicly available genomic and transcriptomic datasets.

The project combines literature review, candidate gene identification, RNA-seq analysis, gene prediction, and functional interpretation to better understand the molecular basis of important plant characteristics.

---

## Research Question

**Which candidate genes and biological pathways contribute to the nutritional and medicinal traits of *Moringa oleifera*?**

---

## Objectives

- Review published evidence on the nutritional and medicinal properties of *Moringa oleifera*.
- Investigate available genomic and transcriptomic resources.
- Identify candidate genes associated with nutritional traits.
- Identify candidate genes associated with medicinal properties.
- Generate genome annotations using computational gene prediction tools.
- Quantify and explore gene expression using RNA-seq data.
- Investigate pathways involved in nutrient biosynthesis and bioactive compound production.
- Prioritize candidate genes for future investigation.

---

## Data Resources

### Reference Genome

- Species: *Moringa oleifera*
- Cultivar: Bhagya
- Assembly: ASM2139783v1
- Accession: GCA_021397835.1

### RNA-Seq Dataset

- Accession: SRR5834758
- Tissue: Young leaf tissue
- Sequencing Type: Paired-end
