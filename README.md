# 🌿 Moringa Genomics Investigation

## Exploring the Genomic Basis of Nutritional and Medicinal Traits in Moringa oleifera: A Bioinformatics Investigation

### Background

Moringa oleifera is widely recognized for its exceptional nutritional value and medicinal properties. This project aims to investigate the genes and biological pathways associated with nutrient biosynthesis, antioxidant activity, and the production of bioactive compounds using bioinformatics approaches.

### Research Question

Which genes and biological pathways contribute to the nutritional and medicinal properties of Moringa oleifera?

### Objectives

- Identify genes associated with nutritional traits.
- Identify genes associated with medicinal traits.
- Perform functional annotation of candidate genes.
- Investigate relevant biological pathways.
- Prioritize candidate genes for future research.
