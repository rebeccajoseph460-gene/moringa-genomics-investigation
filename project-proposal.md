# Exploring the Genomic Basis of Nutritional and Medicinal Traits in Moringa oleifera: A Bioinformatics Investigation

## Background

Moringa oleifera is widely recognized for its exceptional nutritional value and medicinal properties. Its leaves are rich in proteins, vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and bioactive compounds that contribute to its growing importance in food, health, and agricultural systems.

While many nutritional and therapeutic benefits have been reported, the genetic basis underlying these traits remains incompletely understood. Advances in genomics and bioinformatics provide opportunities to investigate genes and biological pathways associated with nutrient biosynthesis, antioxidant production, and other beneficial plant characteristics.

## Research Question

Which genes and biological pathways contribute to the nutritional and medicinal properties of Moringa oleifera?

## Aim

To investigate the genomic basis of nutritional and medicinal traits in Moringa oleifera using bioinformatics approaches and publicly available genomic resources.

## Objectives

### General Objective

To identify and characterize genes associated with nutritional and medicinal traits in Moringa oleifera.

### Specific Objectives

- Identify genes associated with nutritional traits.
- Identify genes associated with medicinal properties.
- Functionally annotate candidate genes.
- Investigate pathways involved in nutrient biosynthesis and bioactive compound production.
- Prioritize candidate genes for future investigation.

## Working Hypothesis

Genes involved in nutrient biosynthesis, antioxidant production, and secondary metabolism contribute to the nutritional and medicinal properties of Moringa oleifera.

## Expected Outcomes

- Literature review
- Candidate gene catalogue
- Functional annotation report
- Pathway analysis
- Final project report
